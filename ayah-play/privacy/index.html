Privacy Policy

Ayah Play: Quran for Kids · by Kevro Apps

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Introduction

Kevro Apps ("we", "our" or "us") made Ayah Play as a calm, offline play space where very young children, roughly ages two to five, listen to short surahs of the Qur'an while they play with gentle on-screen toys.

The app was built to collect nothing. It has no internet access, no advertising, no accounts, no analytics, no purchases and no links out of the app. We believe an app for toddlers should be simple, safe and respectful of family privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what the app does and does not do with information, what permissions it uses, and how we meet privacy and child-safety rules including:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
  • GDPR and GDPR-K (EU data protection laws)
  • CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • Google Play Families Policy

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, rent, share or transmit:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Birthdays or ages
  • Home addresses
  • Photos or videos
  • Voice recordings
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • GPS or precise location data
  • Advertising IDs (AAID)
  • Behavioural analytics
  • Usage tracking
  • Browsing activity
  • Biometric information
  • Social media information
  • User accounts
  • Any personally identifiable information

The app does not require registration, login, profile creation or social media connections, and it contains no third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, social media integrations or tracking tools.

The App Does Not Use the Internet

Ayah Play does not request the Android internet permission. Every recitation clip, picture and sound is included inside the app when it is installed from Google Play. Nothing is downloaded while the app runs, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Because there is no network access, the app cannot transmit information about your child or your device to us or to anyone else.

Information Stored on Your Device Only

The app remembers a small number of parent settings on the device itself so it can pick up where it left off. These are:

  • the listening mode and the selected surah or ayah,
  • the position in the Qur'an journey,
  • the chosen activities, background and scene rotation,
  • whether haptics and continuous play are on, and
  • whether Kid Mode is active.

These settings are ordinary app preferences. They contain no personal information, they never leave the phone or tablet, and they are deleted when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared in Android settings.

Permissions Used by the App

The app requests no Android permissions. It does not request access to:

  • Internet or network state
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Device location or GPS
  • Contacts
  • SMS or text messages
  • Call logs
  • Photos, media or files
  • Bluetooth
  • Background tracking of any kind

Kid Mode and screen pinning

Kid Mode uses Android's built-in app pinning so a small child cannot wander out of the app. Android asks you to confirm pinning the first time, and only a grown-up who passes the parent gate can turn Kid Mode off. App pinning is an Android feature, not a permission, and it does not give the app access to any data.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Ayah Play is designed for very young children and their families.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar child privacy laws by not collecting personal information from anyone, child or adult.

We do not:

  • collect personal information from children,
  • track children across apps or websites,
  • display advertising of any kind,
  • build behavioural profiles,
  • or share children's information with advertisers or data brokers.

All settings, the Kid Mode exit and the app information sit behind a simple parent gate: a sum a child cannot solve. The child's own screen has no buttons, menus, links or text to read.

Parental Rights

Parents and guardians may contact us at any time about privacy.

Depending on local laws, parents may have rights to:

  • request information about what data is collected,
  • request deletion of data,
  • refuse future data collection,
  • or ask questions about how the app works.

Because Ayah Play collects no personal information, there is no personal data for us to provide, delete or manage. The only stored information is the on-device settings described above, which you can remove yourself by uninstalling the app.

Third-Party Services

The app uses no third-party services. It is installed and updated through Google Play, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Qur'an Recitation Credit

The recitation in the app is Muhammad Siddiq al-Minshawi with child repeat, obtained through the Quranic Universal Library. This credit is shown inside the app's parent settings. It has no bearing on privacy, and the recitation is played from the device only.

Data Security

Although the app collects nothing, we still build it responsibly. We keep permissions at zero, give the app no network access, keep every setting on the device, and follow the Google Play Families Policy and child-safety best practice during development.

International Users

Kevro Apps operates globally and the app may be available in many countries. We aim to respect privacy and child-safety law everywhere, including COPPA (United States), GDPR and GDPR-K (European Union), CCPA/CPRA (California) and the Google Play Families Policy. Because no personal information is collected, there is no stored personal data for us to access, export, correct or delete.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app improvements, legal requirements or platform policy changes. If important changes are made, the effective date above will be updated and major changes will be noted in the app's update notes. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means acceptance of the updated policy.
