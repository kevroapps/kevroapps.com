About UCMAS

World's Leading Mental Math Program

UCMAS (Universal Concept of Mental Arithmetic System) is a globally recognized after-school program that uses the abacus to help children develop exceptional mental math skills, concentration, and cognitive abilities. Our Doha center brings this proven methodology to students across Qatar.
