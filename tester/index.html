Help test Qur'aan Class

Qur'aan Class is an Android app for teachers who run a Qur'aan or Arabic class, and for the students and families who learn with them. Google asks for 12 testers before an app like this can be released. You can be one of them.

What the app does today

  • Set homework by surah and ayah, juz or page
  • Keep a daily memorisation record
  • Listen to recorded recitations, then accept them or ask for another go
  • Take the register and keep families up to date
  • Use the whole app in English or Arabic

What you do

  1. Email us the Gmail on your Android phone. We add you to the test and send you a link.
  2. Open the link on your phone, tap Become a tester and install the app. This takes about 5 minutes.
  3. Stay in the test and keep the app installed for 14 days after we reach 12 testers. We will email you the day it starts and the day it ends.

Email us your Gmail

What to try

Teachers: set one piece of homework and mark one register.

Parents and students: join with a code from your teacher. If your teacher is not on the app yet, open it and tell us what you understood.

There is nothing to pay during the test. It is a test version, so you may find rough edges. Please tell us when you do.

Your Gmail is used only to add you to the test. Please do not send us your child's name or details.
