About Kevro Apps

An independent software studio based in Doha, building thoughtful apps, websites, and automation for people and small businesses.

Kevro Apps is a small independent studio founded on the idea that software should be calm, focused, and respectful of your time. We release slowly and deliberately, and each product is built to do one thing well, with no ads, no tracking, and no clutter.

Our first release, Islamic Coloring for Kids, is live on Google Play. It brings Islamic coloring pages, Arabic letters, numbers, Islamic months, and picture puzzles into a calm child-friendly app with no ads. Qur'aan Class, for Qur'aan teachers and the families who learn with them, is in closed testing on Google Play, and Ayah Play, a quiet Qur'aan app for the youngest children, is coming to Google Play.

We have also built websites, landing pages, and automation for small businesses. We are not taking new website clients at the moment.

How we work

Direct and transparent

You work directly with the studio owner. No account managers, no sales calls. Fixed price agreed before we start.

Family-friendly

We understand what matters to families. Our work respects your values, your audience, and your community.

No lock-in

You own everything we build: source code, domain, content. No proprietary platforms, no recurring license fees, no hidden costs.
