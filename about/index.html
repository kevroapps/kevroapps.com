About Kevro Apps

An independent software studio based in Doha, building thoughtful apps, websites, and automation for people and small businesses.

Kevro Apps is a small independent studio founded on the idea that software should be calm, focused, and respectful of your time. We release slowly and deliberately — each product is built to do one thing well, with no ads, no tracking, and no clutter.

Our first release, Islamic Coloring for Kids, is a calm ad-free coloring app rated 5.0 ★ on Google Play. Every review from families around the world has given it five stars — a sign that thoughtful, quiet design still resonates.

Today we also build websites, landing pages, and automation for small businesses. Our clients are owners who want a clean, working online presence without agency overhead or unnecessary complexity.
