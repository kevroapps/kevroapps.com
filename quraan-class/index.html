Qur'aan Class حلقة القرآن

An Android app for teachers who run a Qur'aan or Arabic class, and for the students and families who learn with them. Homework, records and messages stay in one place.

Help us test it → Privacy policy

At a glance

  • Android only
  • In closed testing on Google Play, no public release date yet
  • The whole app works in English and Arabic
  • Students and parents join with a code from the teacher
  • No ads and no tracking

How it works

The teacher sets homework, keeps the memorisation record and marks the register. Students see what is due and send their recitations. Parents follow along and talk with the teacher.

For teachers

  • Set homework by surah and ayah, juz, page, or in your own words
  • Keep a daily memorisation record: new memorisation, recent revision, older revision
  • Listen to recorded recitations and accept them or ask for another go
  • Take the register: present, late, absent or excused
  • Write to one family or send one announcement to the whole class
  • Run a recitation competition, and give certificates and stars
  • Share a monthly or term progress report as a PDF

For students

  • See homework on a calendar
  • Record a recitation and send it to the teacher
  • Collect stickers, stars and certificates in a learning journal

For parents

  • Follow your child's homework, memorisation record and attendance
  • Hear accepted recitations and the teacher's own recitation
  • Tell the teacher in advance when your child will be away
  • Message the teacher in your child's group

Private by design

Students and parents join with a code from their teacher, so no email is needed for them. Recordings are kept for a set time, then removed. A student never sees fees or a parent's contact details, and a child under 16 can record only once a parent has turned recordings on.

Questions

Is Qur'aan Class available to download?

It is in closed testing on Google Play. There is no public release date yet. If you would like to help test it, the steps are on the tester page.

Which languages does it use?

The whole app works in English and Arabic, and each person chooses their own language.

How do students and parents join?

With a code from their teacher, so no email is needed for them.

Are recordings kept?

Recordings are kept for a set time, then removed. A child under 16 can record only once a parent has turned recordings on.

What can students and parents see?

A student never sees fees or a parent's contact details. There are no ads and no tracking.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Kevro apps are Android only. For questions about iPhone, write to support@kevroapps.com.
