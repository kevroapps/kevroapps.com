What children can do

The app is designed to feel unhurried: color, learn, build puzzles, and save artwork without ads or account setup.

Color Islamic and everyday pages

More than 80 pages include the Kaaba, mosques, nature, fruits, clothing, and Free Draw. Children can fill shapes with one tap or use brush, eraser, shapes, text, and glitter tools.

Learn while coloring

All 28 Arabic letters are included free with tap-to-hear pronunciation, alongside 10 Arabic numbers and the 12 Islamic months in calligraphy.

Build picture puzzles

Puzzle Studio has 36 pictures across six worlds, with sliding tiles or classic jigsaw play and difficulty options from 4 pieces up to 36.

Made for family devices

Islamic Coloring for Kids works on phones and tablets in portrait or landscape. It works completely offline, collects no personal information, and does not share data with third parties.

Questions parents ask

Is Islamic Coloring for Kids free?

42 items are free, including every Arabic letter, the Free Draw canvas, two pages in each of the five art collections, and the first item in every pack. Extra packs are one-time purchases: pay once and keep them. Nothing renews. For the prices, see the Google Play listing.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads of any kind, and no subscription.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It works offline once installed.

Is it safe for my child?

There are no accounts or sign-in and no personal information is collected. Purchases, settings and sharing sit behind a grown-up check, and parents can set their own passcode. Kid Mode pins the app to the screen so small hands cannot wander into other apps. The illustrations contain no faces or figures.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Kevro apps are Android only. For questions about iPhone, write to support@kevroapps.com.

What if a purchase does not work?

Refunds and purchase problems go through Google Play, or you can write to support@kevroapps.com.
