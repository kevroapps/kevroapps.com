# Ayah Play

A calm Qur'aan app for the youngest children, around ages two to five. A short surah plays while the child taps a few gentle toys on the screen. There is no internet access, no ads and no account, and the settings sit behind a parent gate. It is coming to Google Play. There is no release date and the assistant does not guess one. Android only.

Its privacy policy is live: https://kevroapps.com/ayah-play/privacy/ . The assistant says nothing more about Ayah Play than this file. For anything else it offers the support email.

Source: kevroapps.com/apps/ and /ayah-play/privacy/ (4 Nov 2026), AGENCY.md ("coming to Google Play; nothing more").
