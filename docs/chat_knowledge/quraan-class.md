# Qur'aan Class (Arabic name: حلقة القرآن)

An Android app for teachers who run a Qur'aan or Arabic class, and for the students and families who learn with them. It is in closed testing on Google Play. There is no public release date and no price to quote. Android only. For questions about iPhone, the assistant offers the support email. The whole app works in English and Arabic, and each person chooses their own language.

How it works: the teacher sets homework, keeps the memorisation record and marks the register. Students see what is due and send their recitations. Parents follow along and talk with the teacher. Homework, records and messages stay in one place.

For teachers: set homework by surah and ayah, juz, page, or in their own words; keep a daily memorisation record (new memorisation, recent revision, older revision); see what is due for revision; listen to recorded recitations and accept them or ask for another go; record how a portion should sound; take the register (present, late, absent or excused); write to one family or send one announcement to the whole class; run a recitation competition; give certificates and stars; share a monthly or term progress report as a PDF; keep a simple record of fees families have paid; lessons show in each family's own time zone with the teacher's lesson link.

For students: see homework on a calendar, record a recitation and send it to the teacher, collect stickers, stars and certificates in a learning journal.

For parents: follow the child's homework, memorisation record and attendance; hear accepted recitations and the teacher's own recitation; tell the teacher in advance when the child will be away; message the teacher in the child's group.

Privacy: students and parents join with a code from their teacher, so no email is needed for them. Recordings are kept for a set time, then removed. A student never sees fees or a parent's contact details. No ads and no tracking. A child under 16 can record only once a parent has turned recordings on. The assistant never asks for a child's name or details. Policy: https://kevroapps.com/quraan-class/privacy/ . Deleting an account: https://kevroapps.com/quraan-class/delete-account/ .

Testing: Qur'aan Class is in a closed test. People who want to help test can email support@kevroapps.com from the Gmail on their Android phone; the page https://kevroapps.com/tester/ explains the steps. The assistant says nothing about test dates or numbers.
